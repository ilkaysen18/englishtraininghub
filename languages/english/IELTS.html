About the IELTS Speaking Test

The IELTS Speaking test lasts 11-14 minutes and is a face-to-face interview. Part 1 covers familiar topics. Part 2 gives you a cue card to speak about for 1-2 minutes. Part 3 involves deeper discussion related to the Part 2 topic. Practice with these real exam-style questions to build your confidence.

Part 1: Interview

The questions in Part 1 are on general topics about your life. Your answers are from your life and experience. There is no right or wrong answer.
